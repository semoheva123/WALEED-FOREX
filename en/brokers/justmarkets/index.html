Verification checklist before registration

  1. Read the client agreement and identify the full legal name of the entity contracting with you.
  2. Match the entity name, license number and website domain in the official register of the claimed regulator.
  3. Confirm that residents of your country are accepted and which products this specific entity offers.
  4. Review the latest risk disclosure, fee schedule, execution, withdrawal and complaints policies.
  5. Check negative-balance protection, margin and close-out terms; do not assume they are globally uniform.
