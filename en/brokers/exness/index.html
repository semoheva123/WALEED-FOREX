The brand lists multiple accounts and platforms, but the binding details are those in the agreement for the entity serving your country. Verify total costs, leverage and regulatory protections before making a decision.

Overview

Exness offers trading services that may include forex and CFDs, with account types and platforms that vary by market and entity. Descriptions such as “high liquidity” or “fast execution” are not guarantees of an execution price or trading outcome.

Check the official website and client agreement to identify the legal entity you would contract with. A brand may operate through multiple entities with different regulatory frameworks and terms.

Account categories to verify

Standard and Standard Cent

Check eligible instruments, account currency, variable spreads, limits and funding terms with the local entity.

Pro, Raw Spread and Zero

Check actual commissions, execution specifications, minimums and eligibility. Trading cost can vary by instrument, account and time.

These account names appear in broker materials; that does not confirm availability in every country. Details can change. Refer to current product pages and agreements.

Independent verification checklist

  1. Identify the legal entity in the client agreement, not just the brand name.
  2. Search for the entity, domain and permitted activity in the relevant regulator’s official register.
  3. Review risk disclosures, margin, close-out and protections that apply to you.
  4. Include spreads, commission, overnight financing, conversion and payment charges in total cost.
  5. Confirm country eligibility, complaints process and withdrawals before depositing funds.
