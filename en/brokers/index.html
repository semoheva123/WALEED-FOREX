How to verify a forex or CFD broker

A brand name or advertised spread is not enough to assess a financial provider. Start with the entity that will contract with you in your country, then verify authorization, product terms and fees in current primary documents.

Before you register

  1. Read the client agreement and identify the contracting legal entity, not only the marketing brand.
  2. Check the name, website domain and registration details in the regulator’s official register; review any warnings.
  3. Confirm that the entity may serve residents of your country. Do not infer this from an affiliated company’s authorization.
  4. Download the current fee schedule and risk disclosure; compare total trading costs, not just an advertised spread.
  5. Understand the legally applicable complaints and compensation arrangements, if any.
  6. Do not send funds to a personal account or an entity that differs from your contract; never share passwords or one-time codes.

Official verification sources

  • UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) Register — search entities and permitted activities.
  • NFA BASIC — information on firms and individuals within U.S. jurisdiction.
  • CySEC investment firms register — entities within Cyprus’s remit.
  • Investor.gov: Investment Professional Background Check — verification and fraud-prevention guidance.

These regulators and tools have defined jurisdictions and are not a complete global directory. Identify the authority relevant to your country and navigate to its official website independently.
