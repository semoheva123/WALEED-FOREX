Vantage: accounts, costs and what to verify

Trading accounts, instruments and fees can vary by country of residence and legal entity. Use this guide as a checklist, then consult Vantage’s official documents and the relevant regulatory register.

VALID VX editorial content · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Overview

Vantage lists trading services that may include derivatives or CFDs depending on the market and entity. Availability of MT4 or MT5, account type and execution terms should be confirmed for the customer’s country.

Do not rely on regulatory logos or descriptions such as “fast execution” alone; verify the contracting legal entity and the scope of its authorization.

Account types listed in available materials

Standard STP

Check how the spread is calculated, whether a separate commission or other charges apply, and current funding requirements.

Raw ECN and Pro ECN

Verify commission per side or volume, eligibility, actual spreads, instruments and the entity providing the account.

These account categories may not be available in every country. Do not rely on historical figures; use the current fee schedule and client agreement.

How to verify independently

  1. Identify the legal entity that will contract with you and maintain the client relationship.
  2. Check the entity name, license number and permitted activity in the competent regulator’s register.
  3. Compare total cost: spread, commission, financing, conversion and any other charges.
  4. Read margin, close-out, execution, withdrawal, complaints and available protection policies.
  5. Confirm country eligibility before sharing data or transferring funds.
