Versus Trade: what should you verify?

Products, terms and account availability depend on residence and legal entity. Do not rely on a registration link or trade name alone; start with client documents and the relevant regulator’s register.

VALID VX editorial content · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Overview

Versus Trade may list multiple trading products and account terms. We do not publish fixed leverage, deposit or spread figures here because values can vary by instrument, account, legal entity and country.

Confirm whether the service available to you involves direct trading or derivatives, and read the product description and capital-loss risks before opening an account.

What to examine

Account and costs

Review spreads, commission, overnight financing, conversion, deposits and withdrawals as they apply to your specific account.

Entity and availability

Identify the contracting company and jurisdiction governing the relationship, and confirm residents of your country are accepted.

Verification steps

  1. Download the client agreement and risk disclosure from the official website.
  2. Search for the legal entity in the official register of the regulator named in the agreement.
  3. Verify the website domain, product type and countries the entity may serve.
  4. Compare all fees, margin and execution terms, balance protection and withdrawals.
  5. If the bank beneficiary or company differs from the agreement, pause and verify with official support.

Useful primary sources

  • Official Versus Trade website — primary source for current terms and entity details.
  • Standard accounts page and Professional accounts page — check availability and current terms before relying on them.
  • FCA Register and CySEC register — examples only; use the register relevant to your entity.
